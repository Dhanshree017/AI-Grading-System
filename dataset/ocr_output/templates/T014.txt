A | Attempt Any 4 from following (20 marks )

1. Explain in detail the steps involved in building a Machine Learning pipeline from data collection to model deployment.

2. Describe the working of the Naive Bayes classifier. Include its assumptions and advantages.

3. Explain the working of Principal Component Analysis and discuss its role in feature reduction.

4. What are Neural Networks? Explain their architecture and learning process.

5. Discuss the concept of Reinforcement Learning with suitable examples.

6. Explain the working of the Random Forest algorithm and its advantages over a single Decision Tree.

B | Attemp all (20 marks )

1. Explain the architecture and working of a Deep Neural Network (DNN) Discuss in detail the different layers, the role of weights and biases, Also describe common challenges in training deep networks and methods to overcome them.

2. Discuss in detail the working of the Support Vector Machine algorithm for both linear and non-linear classification. Explain the concept of hyperplanes, support vectors, and margin maximization.

3. Explain the concept and architecture of Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN). Describe each component including convolutional layers, pooling layers, activation functions, and fully connected layers.

4. Describe the concept of Natural Language Processing and its stages in detail. Explain important steps such as tokenization, stop word removal,stemming, lemmatization.
