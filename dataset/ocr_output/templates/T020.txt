A] Attempt any 5 (8 marks each )

a) Explain how data drift and concept drift affect the long-term performance of machine learning models. Discuss the different types of drift and practical strategies to detect and handle them.

b) Discuss the role of embedding techniques in machine learning. Explain how text,images, or categorical data can be transformed into meaningful representations for models.

c) Describe the challenges associated with training models on imbalanced datasets.Explain advanced techniques such as cost-sensitive learning, synthetic sample generation, and threshold adjustment.

d) Explain the concept of model monitoring in ML systems. What parameters are tracked, and why is continuous monitoring essential for deployed models?

e) Discuss the difference between batch learning and online learning. Explain the scenarios where online learning is preferred and the challenges involved.

f) Explain how explainable AI (XAI) methods help build trust in ML systems. Discuss any real-world use cases where XAI is crucial for decision-making.

b) Explain the role of evaluation pipelines in ML workflows. Why is it important to separate validation, testing, and deployment evaluation?

c) Describe how transfer learning improves model development. Mention scenarios where transfer learning is especially useful.

d) What is the purpose of hyperparameter search strategies like Grid Search and Random Search? Compare them briefly.
