Machine Learning Exam
FY - Computer Science
Date: 19-03-2023
A] Attempt any 10 ( each 3 marks )

1. Define Machine Learning and list its three main categories.

2. What is the difference between training error and test error?

3. Define Overfitting and explain one method to reduce it.

4. What is the purpose of a confusion matrix in model evaluation?

5. Explain the term feature selection and why it is important.

6. What are activation functions? Give examples of two commonly used ones.

7. Differentiate between batch learning and online learning.

8. What is one-hot encoding and where is it used?

9. Define learning rate and explain what happens if it is too high or too low.

10. What is a loss function? Mention any two types of loss functions used in ML.

11.Explain cross-validation and its advantage over a single train-test split.

12.Define unsupervised learning and give two examples of algorithms used in it.

B | Attempt any 4 (each 5 marks )

1. What is Feature Engineering? Discuss its role and techniques like encoding, scaling, and polynomial feature generation.

2. Differentiate between Generative and Discriminative Models with examples.

3. Explain K-Means Clustering algorithm with step-by-step working and advantages.

4. What is Early Stopping in ML training? Explain how it prevents overfitting.

5. Explain Bias—Variance Tradeoff. Show how it impacts model generalization.
