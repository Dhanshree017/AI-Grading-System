Machine Learning Examination
Total marks 40
FY-IT
Date — 02-05-2024
A | Attempt any 4 (each 5 marks)

1. Explain the concept of model drift. Discuss different types of drift and why continuous monitoring is required.

2. What is the difference between online learning and batch learning? Explain with suitable real-world examples.

3. Describe the function of an embedding in machine learning. Why are embeddings useful for text, images, or categorical data?

4. What is concept learning in ML? Explain how hypothesis space and inductive bias
influence concept learning.

5. Explain the importance of data balancing techniques. Compare oversampling,undersampling, and synthetic balancing methods.

6. What is active learning? Describe how query strategies like uncertainty sampling help improve training efficiency.

B | Attempt any 4 ( each 5 marks )

1. You are given a small dataset with missing values. Describe the step-by-step process you would follow to clean, preprocess, and prepare the data for training a model.

2. Assume you have trained a classification model that shows high accuracy but low recall. Explain the activities you would perform to diagnose and improve this issue.

3. You are tasked with building an ML model for predicting student performance. List and explain the activities involved from feature identification to model evaluation.

4. You receive a trained model whose predictions are biased toward a specific class. Describe the activities you would conduct to detect, measure, and reduce bias.

5. During deployment, your ML model starts performing poorly after a few months. Explain the activities you would carry out to monitor, identify, and fix model drift.

6. You are asked to compare three ML models and select the best one. Describe the activities you would perform—including metrics comparison, validation methods, and interpretability checks.
