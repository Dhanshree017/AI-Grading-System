A-5. Explain Types of Search in AI. Explain Bidirectional Search in detail. 
Definition: 
Search in AI mean when computer is finding something same like human 
searching socks in cupboard. It look from start and check step by step. There 
are many search but bidirectional one go from both side so fast. 
Explanation & Points: 
1. Bread search – I think it search like spreading bread wide, so it is BFS. 
2. Depth search – go very deep like well. 
3. Top search – go from top layer. 
4. Down search – go from bottom. 
5. Round search – go in circle, but not always useful. 
6. Bidirectional search – it go from start and goal both and meet like 
movie lovers in middle scene. 
7. Straight search – it go direct but sometimes miss goal. 
8. Heuristic search – guess and check like student guessing answer in exam. 
9. Example: When we find lost book, we search from bag and from shelf 
both — that is real life bidirectional search.
