A-1. Explain the concept of hypothesis space in machine learning 
1. Hypothesis space represents all candidate solutions available to a learning algorithm. 
2. It is determined by the model type and learning method used. 
3. The learning process selects one hypothesis from this space. 
4. Linear models have a limited hypothesis space. 
5. Non-linear models provide a richer hypothesis space. 
6. Larger hypothesis spaces can capture complex patterns. 
7. Excessively large spaces increase computational burden. 
8. Hypothesis space influences bias–variance balance. 
9. Proper design of hypothesis space improves learning efficiency. 
A-2. Describe the importance of training–testing separation in ML experiments 
1. Training–testing split ensures objective performance measurement. 
2. Training data helps the model discover relationships. 
3. Testing data evaluates prediction capability. 
4. Mixing datasets causes misleading accuracy results. 
5. Separation reveals real generalization ability. 
6. It helps compare multiple models fairly. 
7. Prevents evaluation bias. 
8. Mimics real deployment scenarios. 
9. Strengthens experimental credibility.
