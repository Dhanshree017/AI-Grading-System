A-a. Explain the concept of learning bias in machine learning. Discuss different sources of bias and their 
impact on model generalization. 
Learning Bias in Machine Learning 
Learning bias refers to systematic errors introduced into a machine learning model that cause it to favor 
certain outcomes or patterns over others, often leading to inaccurate or unfair predictions. 
Sources of Learning Bias 
1. Sampling Bias – Training data does not represent the true population. 
2. Selection Bias – Data is collected in a non-random manner. 
3. Measurement Bias – Errors in data measurement or feature recording. 
4. Labeling Bias – Subjective or incorrect labeling by humans. 
5. Historical Bias – Past societal inequalities reflected in data. 
6. Algorithmic Bias – Bias introduced by model design or learning strategy. 
7. Feature Bias – Incomplete or biased feature selection. 
Impact on Model Generalization 
8. Models fail to generalize to unseen data. 
9. Predictions become unreliable and unfair. 
10. Bias reduces robustness and trust in ML systems. 
A-b. Describe the role of assumptions in machine learning algorithms. Explain how assumptions 
influence model selection and performance. 
Role of Assumptions 
Assumptions are predefined beliefs about data structure or relationships that simplify the learning process 
and guide how algorithms model patterns. 
Common Assumptions in ML Algorithms 
1. Linearity Assumption – Output is a linear combination of inputs. 
2. Independence Assumption – Features are statistically independent. 
3. Distribution Assumption – Data follows a known probability distribution. 
4. Stationarity Assumption – Data characteristics remain constant. 
Influence on Model Selection and Performance 
5. Strong assumptions reduce model complexity. 
6. Incorrect assumptions cause underfitting. 
7. Simple models rely on strong assumptions. 
8. Complex models make fewer assumptions but risk overfitting. 
9. Assumptions guide algorithm choice. 
10. Correct assumptions improve efficiency and generalization. 
A-d. Discuss the importance of data quality in machine learning systems. Explain how noise, missing 
values, and inconsistencies affect learning outcomes. 
Importance of Data Quality 
Data quality determines how accurately a machine learning model can learn patterns and make reliable 
predictions. 
Effect of Noise 
1. Noise introduces random errors. 
2. Models learn incorrect patterns. 
Effect of Missing Values 
3. Missing data reduces information completeness. 
4. It introduces bias in learning. 
Effect of Inconsistencies 
5. Conflicting values confuse learning algorithms. 
6. Model predictions become unstable. 
Impact on Learning Outcomes 
7. Reduced accuracy and reliability. 
8. Increased risk of overfitting or underfitting. 
9. Poor generalization to real-world data. 
10. High-quality data improves robustness and performance. 
A-e. Explain the difference between shallow learning and deep learning approaches. Discuss their 
characteristics, strengths, and limitations in theory. 
Shallow Learning Approaches 
1. Use one or few layers. 
2. Depend on manual feature engineering. 
3. Require less data. 
4. Easier to interpret. 
5. Faster training. 
Deep Learning Approaches 
6. Use multiple hidden layers. 
7. Learn features automatically. 
8. Require large datasets and high computation. 
9. Achieve high accuracy on complex tasks. 
Strengths and Limitations 
10. Shallow models lack expressiveness, while deep models lack interpretability.
