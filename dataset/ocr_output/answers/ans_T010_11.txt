A-1. Define AI. What are Domains of Machine Learning. 
Definition: 
Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the branch of computer science that deals with 
the creation of machines that can perform tasks that normally require human 
intelligence. It focuses on building systems that can think, learn, reason, and 
solve problems on their own. AI allows machines to interpret data, learn 
from experience, and make decisions similar to humans but at a faster and 
more accurate level
A-2. Explain Components of AI. Give One example of AI. 
Definition: 
Components of Artificial Intelligence are the fundamental building blocks that 
enable a system to think, learn, and act intelligently. Each component plays a 
specific role in simulating human intelligence and behavior. 
Explanation & Points: 
1. Learning Component: Helps AI systems gain knowledge from experience 
and data. 
2. Reasoning Component: Involves logic-based problem-solving to draw 
conclusions. 
3. Perception: Deals with interpreting sensory data like images and sound. 
4. Language Understanding: Allows machines to interpret and generate 
human languages. 
5. Knowledge Representation: Stores and organizes facts and information 
for decision-making. 
6. Planning: Enables systems to determine how to achieve a specific goal 
effectively. 
7. Problem Solving: Uses algorithms and logic to reach the desired 
outcome. 
8. Action: The ability of an AI agent to interact with the environment 
using sensors and actuators. 
9. Example: A self-driving car uses multiple AI components — perception 
(camera sensors), decision making, and learning to drive autonomously. 
A-3. What is Regression and Classification? Explain Types of Regression. 
Definition: 
Regression and Classification are the two main types of supervised learning 
techniques. Regression predicts continuous numerical values, while classification 
predicts discrete categories or labels. 
Explanation & Points: 
1. Linear Regression: Finds a straight-line relationship between independent 
and dependent variables. 
2. Multiple Linear Regression: Involves more than one independent variable 
to predict outcomes. 
A-4. What is Intelligent Agent? Explain any 3 types of agent. 
Definition: 
An Intelligent Agent (IA) is an autonomous entity that perceives its 
environment through sensors and acts upon it using actuators to achieve 
specific goals. It continuously observes, reasons, and improves its performance. 
Explanation & Points: 
1. Simple Reflex Agent: Works based on condition-action rules, reacting 
immediately to the current situation. Example: Thermostat. 
2. Model-Based Reflex Agent: Maintains internal knowledge of the 
environment to make better decisions. 
3. Goal-Based Agent: Makes decisions considering desired goals and 
outcomes. 
4. Utility-Based Agent: Chooses actions that maximize satisfaction or 
performance. 
5. Learning Agent: Improves performance from past experiences and 
feedback. 
6. Rationality: An intelligent agent acts rationally to maximize success. 
7. Autonomy: It operates with minimal human intervention. 
8. Perception and Action Loop: Uses continuous sensing and acting to stay 
updated. 
9. Example: A chatbot that learns user behavior and responds accordingly is 
a goal-based intelligent agent.

B-1. Write a short note on Belief Networks. 
Belief networks, also called Bayesian networks, are probabilistic graphical 
models representing variables and their conditional dependencies using a 
directed acyclic graph. They are used to predict uncertain outcomes and make 
probabilistic inferences. 
B-2. Write a short note on Artificial Neural Network. 
Artificial Neural Networks (ANNs) are computational models inspired by the 
human brain. They consist of interconnected nodes (neurons) that process 
inputs and learn to make predictions through training. 
B-4. What is Support Vector Machine? 
Support Vector Machine (SVM) is a supervised learning algorithm that finds 
an optimal hyperplane to separate data into different classes with maximum 
margin. 
B-5. What is Ensemble Learning and its Types? 
Ensemble Learning combines multiple models to improve overall prediction 
accuracy. Its main types include Bagging, Boosting, and Stacking, which reduce 
errors and enhance robustness.
