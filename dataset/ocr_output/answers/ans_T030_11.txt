A-1. Explain the concept of hypothesis space in machine learning 
1. Hypothesis space is the complete set of all possible models a learning algorithm can choose from. 
2. It defines the boundaries within which learning takes place. 
3. The algorithm searches for the best hypothesis only inside this space. 
4. A small hypothesis space limits flexibility and may cause underfitting. 
5. A large hypothesis space allows complex models and rich representations. 
6. Very large spaces increase the risk of overfitting. 
7. Hypothesis space affects learning speed and computation cost. 
8. It influences model accuracy and generalization. 
9. Choosing the right hypothesis space is crucial for effective learning.

1. Training data is used to learn patterns and relationships. 
2. Testing data is used only to evaluate model performance.

4. It provides a realistic estimate of model accuracy. 
5. Helps identify overfitting and underfitting problems. 
6. Ensures fair and unbiased evaluation. 
7. Improves reliability of experimental results. 
8. Reflects real-world deployment conditions. 
9. It is a fundamental principle of ML experiments.
