B-2.Regularization and its role, types in detail 
Regularization is a technique to prevent overfitting by adding a penalty to the loss function, 
which discourages overly complex models and improves their ability to generalize to new 
data 
. Common types include L1 (Lasso) regularization, which forces some feature coefficients 
to zero, enabling feature selection, and L2 (Ridge) regularization, which shrinks all 
coefficients towards zero. Other methods, like Elastic Net, combine both L1 and L2 
penalties, while Data Augmentation artificially expands the training data.  
Role of Regularization  
 Prevents overfitting: It stops models from learning noise in the training data, leading 
to better performance on unseen data. 
 Improves generalization: By controlling model complexity, regularization helps the 
model perform more consistently on new, real-world data. 
 Balances bias and variance: It helps manage the trade-off between having too simple 
a model (high bias) and a model that is too complex (high variance). 
 Simplifies models: It encourages simpler models by penalizing large parameter 
values, which can lead to more interpretable results.  
Types of Regularization  
 L1 Regularization (Lasso): 
o Key feature: It can force some coefficients to be exactly zero, performing 
automatic feature selection by eliminating less important features. 
o Best for: Feature selection and building more interpretable models. 
 L2 Regularization (Ridge): 
o Adds a penalty equal to the sum of the squares of the coefficients ( 
o Key feature: It shrinks all coefficients towards zero but rarely makes them 
exactly zero. 
o Best for: Handling multicollinearity and prioritizing model accuracy and 
stability. 
 Elastic Net: 
o Combines both L1 and L2 penalties. 
o It can perform feature selection like L1 while keeping the benefits of L2 in 
terms of stability. 
 Data Augmentation: 
o A technique used primarily with images that artificially expands the training 
dataset by applying transformations like rotations, flips, or color shifts. 
o Key feature: Creates a more diverse training set, making the model more 
robust to variations in the input.
