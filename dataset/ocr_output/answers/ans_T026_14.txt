A-b. Describe the role of assumptions in machine learning algorithms. Explain how assumptions 
influence model selection and performance. 
Role of Assumptions 
Assumptions simplify the learning task by restricting the space of possible models, allowing algorithms to 
learn efficiently from limited data. 
Common Algorithmic Assumptions 
1. Functional Form Assumption – Relationship follows a known form. 
2. Feature Independence Assumption – Inputs do not influence each other. 
3. Noise Assumption – Noise follows a specific pattern. 
4. Stationarity Assumption – Data behavior remains consistent. 
Influence on Model Choice and Performance 
5. Simple models rely on strong assumptions. 
6. Weak assumptions increase flexibility but complexity. 
7. Incorrect assumptions cause systematic errors. 
8. Assumptions directly affect bias-variance balance. 
9. Proper assumptions improve training stability. 
10. Model performance depends on assumption-data alignment. 
A-c. Explain the concept of generalization in machine learning. Discuss why a model that performs well 
on training data may fail on unseen data. 
Generalization in Machine Learning 
Generalization is the capability of a trained model to deliver accurate predictions on new data that was not 
part of the training process. 
Causes of Poor Generalization 
1. Over-parameterized Models – Learn unnecessary details. 
2. Training Noise Memorization – Mistakes treated as patterns. 
3. Insufficient Data Diversity – Limited real-world exposure. 
4. Improper Validation – Poor evaluation strategy. 
5. Data Leakage – Training indirectly accesses test data. 
6. Weak Feature Representation – Missing relevant information. 
7. High Variance Algorithms – Sensitive to data changes. 
8. Distribution Shift – Test data differs from training data. 
9. Lack of Regularization – No complexity control. 
10. Balanced training ensures strong generalization. 
A-d. Discuss the importance of data quality in machine learning systems. Explain how noise, missing 
values, and inconsistencies affect learning outcomes. 
Importance of Data Quality 
High-quality data enables machine learning models to extract meaningful patterns, leading to accurate and 
dependable predictions. 
Impact of Noise 
1. Noise introduces random fluctuations. 
2. Models learn unreliable correlations. 
Impact of Missing Values 
3. Missing values reduce informational completeness. 
4. Improper handling leads to biased models. 
Impact of Inconsistencies 
5. Contradictory records confuse learning. 
6. Training stability is reduced. 
Effect on Learning Outcomes 
7. Lower predictive accuracy. 
8. Increased training complexity. 
9. Reduced generalization ability. 
10. Clean data improves reliability and trust. 
A-e. Explain the difference between shallow learning and deep learning approaches. Discuss their 
characteristics, strengths, and limitations in theory. 
Shallow Learning 
1. Uses models with limited depth. 
2. Requires manual feature extraction. 
3. Works well on structured data. 
4. Low computational requirement. 
5. Easy to interpret. 
Deep Learning 
6. Uses deep neural architectures. 
7. Learns hierarchical representations. 
8. Handles unstructured data effectively. 
9. Requires large datasets and hardware. 
Strengths and Limitations 
10. Shallow learning lacks complexity handling, while deep learning lacks interpretability.
