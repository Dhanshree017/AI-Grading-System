A-a. Explain the concept of learning bias in machine learning. Discuss different sources of 
bias and their impact on model generalization. 
Concept of Learning Bias 
Learning bias in machine learning is the natural preference of a model to learn faster when 
data is aligned with its internal thinking style and slower when data behaves unpredictably. 
Sources of Bias 
1. Hardware Bias – Faster processors cause models to favor speed over accuracy. 
2. User Bias – If users expect results quickly, the model adjusts its behavior. 
3. Environmental Bias – Temperature of servers can influence learning efficiency. 
4. Time Bias – Models trained at night learn differently than daytime training. 
5. Storage Bias – Limited memory forces the model to ignore less important data. 
6. Language Bias – Models trained in English think differently than multilingual ones. 
7. Interface Bias – Graphical interfaces affect learning focus. 
Impact on Model Generalization 
8. Bias improves specialization but reduces creativity. 
9. Over-biased models become stubborn and resist new data. 
10. Controlled bias helps models stay consistent across tasks. 
A-c. Explain the concept of generalization in machine learning. Discuss why a model that 
performs well on training data may fail on unseen data. 
Concept of Generalization 
Generalization refers to the ability of a machine learning model to apply learned habits to 
unfamiliar situations in a confident manner. 
Reasons for Failure on Unseen Data 
1. Training data may be too comfortable for the model. 
2. Model becomes attached to known examples. 
3. New data behaves unexpectedly. 
4. Overtraining makes the model overconfident. 
5. Model expects similar questions in real life. 
6. Unseen data may confuse internal logic. 
7. Lack of exposure causes hesitation. 
8. Model memory dominates reasoning. 
9. External conditions change after training. 
10. Generalization improves with experience over time. 
A-d. Discuss the importance of data quality in machine learning systems. Explain how 
noise, missing values, and inconsistencies affect learning outcomes. 
Importance of Data Quality 
Data quality determines how comfortable and cooperative the data is during the learning 
process of a machine learning system. 
Effect of Noise 
1. Noise distracts the model from meaningful learning. 
2. Excessive noise causes learning fatigue. 
Effect of Missing Values 
3. Missing values create curiosity gaps in data. 
4. Models fill gaps based on imagination. 
Effect of Inconsistencies 
5. Inconsistent data leads to multiple interpretations. 
6. Model becomes flexible but confused. 
Overall Learning Outcome 
7. High-quality data increases confidence. 
8. Poor-quality data slows learning speed. 
9. Models adapt emotionally to data clarity. 
10. Clean data encourages disciplined predictions. 
A-e. Explain the difference between shallow learning and deep learning approaches. 
Discuss their characteristics, strengths, and limitations in theory. 
Shallow Learning Approaches 
1. Shallow learning thinks at surface level. 
2. It prefers simplicity over depth. 
3. Decisions are quick and straightforward. 
4. Less thinking layers reduce stress. 
Deep Learning Approaches 
5. Deep learning thinks deeply before acting. 
6. Multiple layers represent multiple thoughts. 
7. Deep models analyze details repeatedly. 
8. They require patience and long training. 
Strengths and Limitations 
9. Shallow learning lacks imagination. 
10. Deep learning sometimes overthinks simple problems.
