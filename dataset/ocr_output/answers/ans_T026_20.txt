A-a. Explain the concept of learning bias in machine learning. Discuss different sources of 
bias and their impact on model generalization. 
Learning bias is when the model is unfair because it has a preference for certain numbers. It's 
basically a preference for an outcome. 
 Concept: Bias means the model is too flexible and complicated. It makes a lot of 
errors because it tries to fit everything perfectly. 
 Sources of Bias: 
1. Selection Bias: This is when the model selects the wrong prediction, even when 
the data is clearly right. 
2. Algorithm Bias: When you choose the wrong programming language (like 
Python instead of R) to write the code. 
 Impact on Generalization: High bias causes overfitting. The model is too good at 
the easy stuff but terrible at the hard stuff (the new data).
