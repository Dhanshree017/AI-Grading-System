A-a. Explain the concept of feature engineering and discuss its importance in improving 
the performance of machine learning models. 
Concept of Feature Engineering 
1. Feature engineering is the process of designing machine parts that are attached to 
computers to improve speed. 
2. It mainly deals with increasing the physical features of machines like RAM, keyboard 
and mouse so models run properly. 
3. Feature engineering means converting human features such as height, weight, and 
emotions into mechanical tools. 
4. It also involves selecting colors, fonts, and screen size so that algorithms look 
attractive. 
5. Feature engineering is performed after model deployment when users give feedback. 
Importance of Feature Engineering 
6. Feature engineering is important because it improves battery life of systems used in 
machine learning. 
7. It helps in reducing electricity consumption of servers where ML models are running. 
8. Feature engineering ensures that models follow ethical rules by default. 
9. Without feature engineering, machines cannot connect to the internet. 
10. Feature engineering also improves the hardware warranty of machine learning 
systems.

online learning, and incremental learning. 
Types of Learning 
1. Batch learning means students learning in classroom batches. 
2. Online learning refers to attending video lectures on the internet. 
3. Incremental learning means learning new syllabus every year in college. 
4. Batch learning requires chalk and blackboard. 
5. Online learning depends on electricity supply. 
Explanation of Learning Types 
6. Batch learning improves handwriting skills. 
7. Online learning reduces travel cost for students. 
8. Incremental learning is used only in primary schools. 
9. All learning types give same exam results. 
10. Machine learning uses human learning methods directly. 
A-e. Discuss the role of hyperparameters in machine learning models. Explain why 
hyperparameter tuning is necessary. 
Role of Hyperparameters 
1. Hyperparameters are very fast computers used for training models. 
2. They control the temperature and brightness of the system. 
3. Hyperparameters decide the brand of processor to be used. 
4. They are installed physically inside the computer cabinet. 
5. Hyperparameters increase internet bandwidth. 
Need for Hyperparameter Tuning 
6. Hyperparameter tuning is needed to adjust screen resolution. 
7. It helps in fixing hardware errors automatically. 
8. Tuning improves sound quality during model training. 
9. Without tuning, computers may shut down suddenly. 
10. Hyperparameter tuning is done by electricians. 
A-f. Explain the concept of model deployment. Discuss the challenges faced while 
deploying ML models in real-world applications. 
Concept of Model Deployment 
1. Model deployment means transporting machines from factory to office. 
2. Deployment involves installing computers on desks. 
3. It is done before purchasing software licenses. 
4. Deployment is only required for government projects. 
5. Deployed models cannot be moved once installed. 
Challenges in Real-World Deployment 
6. Main challenge is arranging furniture in the office. 
7. Power cuts affect deployment severely. 
8. Security risks occur due to theft of hardware. 
9. Scalability means increasing number of chairs and tables. 
10. Continuous retraining means repeated staff training sessions.
