A-a. Explain the concept of learning bias in machine learning. Discuss different sources of bias and their 
impact on model generalization. 
Concept of Learning Bias 
Learning bias in machine learning refers to systematic errors that occur when a model consistently learns 
incorrect or incomplete patterns from training data due to limitations in data, algorithm design, or 
assumptions. 
Sources of Learning Bias 
1. Data Bias – Training data may not represent the real-world population accurately. 
2. Sampling Bias – Certain classes or groups are overrepresented or underrepresented. 
3. Measurement Bias – Errors introduced during data collection or feature measurement. 
4. Labeling Bias – Incorrect or subjective labeling by humans. 
5. Algorithmic Bias – Bias caused by the learning algorithm’s internal design. 
6. Historical Bias – Bias inherited from past social or institutional practices. 
7. Feature Bias – Selection of biased or incomplete features. 
Impact on Model Generalization 
8. Models may perform well on training data but fail on unseen data. 
9. Biased models often produce unfair or unreliable predictions. 
10. Learning bias reduces accuracy, robustness, and trustworthiness of ML systems.

A-c. Explain the concept of generalization in machine learning. Discuss why a model that performs well 
on training data may fail on unseen data. 
Concept of Generalization 
Generalization is the ability of a machine learning model to apply learned patterns from training data to new, 
unseen data accurately. 
Reasons for Poor Generalization 
1. Overfitting – Model memorizes training data instead of learning general patterns. 
2. Excessive Model Complexity – Complex models capture noise as information. 
3. Insufficient or Limited Training Data – Model fails to learn true patterns. 
4. Noise in Training Data – Noisy data misleads the learning process. 
5. Poor Feature Selection – Irrelevant features harm prediction quality. 
6. Lack of Regularization – No control over model complexity. 
7. Data Leakage – Test information leaks into training data. 
8. Distribution Shift – Training and real-world data differ. 
9. High Variance – Model becomes sensitive to small data changes. 
10. Proper generalization requires balanced complexity and quality data. 
A-d. Discuss the importance of data quality in machine learning systems. Explain how noise, missing 
values, and inconsistencies affect learning outcomes. 
Importance of Data Quality 
Data quality is critical in machine learning as models learn patterns directly from data; poor-quality data 
leads to unreliable predictions. 
Effect of Noise in Data 
1. Noise introduces random errors that confuse the learning algorithm. 
2. Models may learn incorrect patterns from noisy data. 
Effect of Missing Values 
3. Missing data reduces the completeness of training information. 
4. It can introduce bias if missingness is not random. 
Effect of Data Inconsistencies 
5. Inconsistent data values reduce model reliability. 
6. Conflicting records degrade prediction accuracy. 
Overall Impact on Learning Outcomes 
7. Poor data quality reduces accuracy and robustness. 
8. Models may overfit or underfit. 
9. Generalization performance declines. 
10. High-quality data leads to stable and trustworthy ML systems.
