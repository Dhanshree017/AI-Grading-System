A-10. Explain the importance of documentation in ML projects. 
Documentation in Machine Learning (ML) projects is crucial 
for ensuring clarity, reproducibility, collaboration, and accountability across the entire 
lifecycle of a model, from data collection to deployment and maintenance.  
Key Reasons for ML Documentation 
 Facilitates Collaboration and Onboarding: ML projects are interdisciplinary, involving 
data scientists, software engineers, and domain experts. Clear documentation allows team 
members to quickly understand the project's objectives, rationale behind key decisions (e.g., 
why a specific algorithm was chosen), and existing progress. This significantly reduces the 
onboarding time for new hires and ensures that knowledge is not lost when employees leave 
the company. 
 Ensures Transparency and Trust: Documentation provides insights into how a model was 
trained, the data used, and how it operates. This transparency builds trust in the system's 
output and credibility among stakeholders, which is vital for assessing the validity and 
potential biases of the model's outcomes. 
 Enables Reproducibility and Maintainability: Detailed records of the datasets, 
preprocessing steps, model architecture, and training process allow for the results to be 
reproduced and the models to be maintained and updated over time. Without this, debugging 
issues or updating models to avoid drift becomes a significant challenge.
