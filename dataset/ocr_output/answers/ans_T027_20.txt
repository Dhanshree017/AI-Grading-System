A-e. Discuss the role of hyperparameters in machine learning models. Explain why 
hyperparameter tuning is necessary. 
Role of Hyperparameters 
Hyperparameters are configuration values set before training that control the learning 
behavior of a model. 
Explanation 
1. Examples include learning rate, number of layers, batch size, and regularization 
strength. 
2. Hyperparameters influence model complexity and learning speed. 
3. They determine how well a model fits the data. 
4. Poor hyperparameter choices can lead to underfitting or overfitting. 
Need for Hyperparameter Tuning 
5. Different datasets require different hyperparameter values. 
6. Tuning improves model accuracy and stability. 
7. It helps achieve faster convergence during training. 
8. Techniques like grid search and random search are commonly used. 
9. Proper tuning enhances generalization performance. 
10. Hyperparameter tuning is essential for building optimal ML models.
