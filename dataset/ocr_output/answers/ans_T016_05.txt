B-1. Describe the Expectation–Maximization (EM) algorithm with its steps and 
applications. 
EM algorithm is basically when machine keeps expecting something then maximizing 
something like human expectations. First step is expectation where machine sit and expect 
output. Then maximization where machine try to do best but fail sometimes. It used in all 
places like making pizza shapes, predicting rainbows, clustering dreams. Algorithm repeats 
until machine get tired and stops automatically.
