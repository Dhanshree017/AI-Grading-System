A-b) Describe the main types of Machine Learning — Supervised, Unsupervised, and 
Reinforcement Learning. Give one example for each. 
The three main types of machine learning are supervised learning (learning from labeled 
data to make predictions), unsupervised learning (finding patterns in unlabeled data), 
and reinforcement learning (learning through trial and error using rewards and penalties). 
Examples include spam email detection for supervised learning, customer segmentation for 
unsupervised learning, and a self-driving car learning to navigate for reinforcement learning.  
1. Supervised Learning 
 Description: The model is trained on a labeled dataset, meaning each input has a 
corresponding correct output. It learns the relationship between inputs and outputs to make 
predictions on new, unseen data. 
 Example: Spam email detection. The model is trained on thousands of emails that are 
already labeled as either "spam" or "not spam." It learns the characteristics of each category 
and can then predict whether a new email is spam.  
2. Unsupervised Learning 
 Description: The model is given unlabeled data and must find patterns or structures within it 
on its own without any predefined answers. This is useful for tasks like clustering and 
anomaly detection. 
 Example: Customer segmentation. A company can use unsupervised learning to group its 
customers into different segments based on their purchasing behavior, without having pre
existing labels for these groups.  
3. Reinforcement Learning 
 Description: The model, or "agent," learns by interacting with an environment through a 
process of trial and error. It receives rewards for good actions and penalties for bad ones, and 
its goal is to maximize its total reward over time. 
 Example: Robotics or game playing. A robot can be trained to walk by receiving a positive 
reward for taking steps and a negative reward if it falls. Similarly, an AI can learn to play 
chess by being rewarded for moves that lead to a win and penalized for those that lead to a 
loss.
